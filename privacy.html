# Privacy Policy — Pigeon Revenge **Last updated: November 4, 2026** Pigeon Revenge is a mobile arcade game developed by Guano Devops. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Pigeon Revenge. ## 1. Information We Process Pigeon Revenge stores game progress, unlocked levels, settings and scores locally on your device. The game also integrates Google Play Games Services and Google AdMob. These third-party services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies. ## 2. Google Play Games Services Pigeon Revenge uses Google Play Games Services to provide player authentication and online leaderboards. When you use these features, Google may process your player identifier, profile information, gameplay scores and related service data. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/privacy ## 3. Advertising — Google AdMob Pigeon Revenge uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including interstitial and rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions and diagnostic information, depending on device settings, applicable law and consent choices. Where required, the game requests advertising consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Users may be offered personalized, non-personalized or limited advertising according to the available choices and applicable requirements. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads ## 4. Local Game Data Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Removing the application may delete this information. Online leaderboard scores and related account data may be maintained by Google Play Games Services. ## 5. Data Sharing Guano Devops does not sell player information. Information may be processed by Google through the integrated Play Games Services and AdMob SDKs for the purposes described above. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Local game data remains on your device until it is removed, reset or deleted through the operating system or game functionality, where available. Data processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion policies. For privacy-related requests concerning Pigeon Revenge, contact us at leonardo.capitani@gmail.com. ## 7. Children's Privacy Pigeon Revenge is intended for the audience declared in its Google Play listing. Advertising and privacy features are configured according to applicable platform policies and age-related requirements. ## 8. Your Privacy Choices Where applicable, users can review or change their advertising privacy choices through the privacy options provided in the game. You may also manage certain Google account and advertising preferences through your Google account settings. ## 9. Security We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game. Third-party services maintain their own security practices. ## 10. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when game features, third-party services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page. ## 11.
