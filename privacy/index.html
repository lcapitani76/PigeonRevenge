01

Who we are & what this covers

Pigeon Revenge is a mobile arcade game developed by Guano Devops. This policy explains how information is handled in connection with the game, its Google services and privacy enquiries.

The game uses local storage for gameplay data, Google Play Games Services for player sign-in and leaderboards, and Google AdMob for advertising. The information processed by Google depends on the service, SDK configuration, device and account settings.

02

Your progress, saved locally

The game stores progress, unlocked levels, settings and local scores on your device so you can continue playing and keep your preferences.

You can remove local app data through your device’s app settings. Clearing app storage can permanently erase your progress. Device or operating-system backup settings may affect whether app data is backed up or restored.

Local saves are separate from scores submitted to Google Play Games. Clearing local storage does not, by itself, delete information held by Google.

03

Google Play Games Services

Play Games supports player authentication and online leaderboards in Pigeon Revenge. It may process your player identifier, gamer profile information and submitted scores to identify your profile and rank your results.

Your gamer name, profile image and score may be visible to other players through leaderboards, depending on the feature and your Google profile settings. Google also handles service-related technical data under its own privacy policy.

Manage your profile visibility, sign-in preferences and game data using Google Play Games settings. Google provides tools to delete data for individual games or your Play Games profile.

04

Google AdMob & advertising consent

Pigeon Revenge uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. These data support ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention; an IP address may also be used to estimate general location.

Advertising data use, including any personalization, depends on the app’s advertising configuration, applicable requirements and your settings. Details about Google’s processing and controls are available in the links below.

Current consent status

Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) is not yet integrated into Pigeon Revenge. This policy does not indicate that an in-game UMP consent form or privacy-options control is available.

Reading this page does not give consent to advertising data processing. Device and Google advertising settings do not replace an in-game consent process where one is required.

05

Your choices & privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

  • Local gameplay data: manage or erase it in your device’s app-storage settings.
  • Play Games information: use Google’s profile and game-data controls linked above.
  • Advertising: review your device’s advertising privacy controls and Google ad preferences. These controls may not stop all advertising or all related data processing.
  • Requests to Guano Devops: email the address below and describe your request. Avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Contact information & retention

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use these details to respond to and handle your enquiry, including applicable privacy obligations. The relevant legal basis depends on the request and applicable law, such as our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries or compliance with a legal obligation.

Enquiry information is kept for as long as needed to handle the matter and meet applicable legal obligations. Local data remains subject to your device’s storage and backup controls. Google determines retention for data it processes under its policies and your account settings; no single retention period is stated here for all Google data.

Third-party processing

Google processes information through its infrastructure, which may involve countries outside your own. Its privacy policy explains its international-transfer safeguards. Contact us about data handled by Guano Devops; for data managed by Google, use Google’s privacy tools or contact Google. Deleting Play Games data does not automatically delete correspondence sent to us.

06

Keeping this policy up to date

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the game, connected services or privacy practices. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Any future consent-management integration will need to be reflected in the game and in this policy.
